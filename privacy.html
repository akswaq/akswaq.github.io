Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how Paisa aur AI ("we") handles information on this website and in the Paisa aur AI uploader app.

1. Visitors to this website

  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or sign-up forms.
  • We do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

2. The Paisa aur AI uploader app

The uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Paisa aur AI channel. It is not offered to the public.

  • What it connects to: the owner's own YouTube channel (through Google sign-in) and the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account (through Meta sign-in).
  • What it uses: the channel or Page name and ID, and permission to upload videos and publish posts. It uses this only to publish the channel's own videos with their captions when the owner presses a button.
  • What it stores: the sign-in tokens, kept only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any server of ours.
  • What it never does: it does not sell, share, transfer or use any data for advertising, and it does not access data about other people.

3. Google and YouTube

  • The uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Paisa aur AI uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions.

4. Facebook and Instagram

  • Meta's handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Access can be removed at any time in Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations. See also our data deletion instructions.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept only while the connection is needed and are deleted from the owner's computer when the connection is removed. To ask about or delete any data, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

6. Children

This website and the uploader are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
