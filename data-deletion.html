Data deletion instructions

The Paisa aur AI uploader stores no data about anyone except the channel owner's own sign-in tokens, which are kept only on the owner's computer.

Remove the app from Facebook or Instagram

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Find Paisa aur AI uploader and press Remove.

Remove the app from Google / YouTube

Open Google account permissions, choose Paisa aur AI uploader and press Delete all connections.

Ask us to delete data

Email akswaq99@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete any related data and confirm by email within 30 days.
